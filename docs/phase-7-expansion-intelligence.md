# Phase 7 — Expansion Intelligence

Phase 7 converts measured organization usage into dashboard-first growth recommendations.

## Signals

- conversation capacity growth
- specialist-agent opportunity
- lead-operations expansion
- human-handoff review
- automation expansion
- second-channel evaluation

## Guardrails

- no pricing-plan dependency
- no automatic upsell email
- no automatic agent/channel/workflow changes
- recommendations require measured operational evidence
- human review is required before commercial or operational expansion

## Dashboard

Route: `/dashboard/expansion`

The dashboard displays organization-level opportunity score, priority, confidence, evidence, and recommended next action.
